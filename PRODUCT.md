# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Owners of a self-hosted Ghost Pod plus phone-local users with no Pod. They talk to Ghost daily, in short private moments, to think, remember, and act. Primary task on any screen is one verb: talk, approve, review, or connect.

## Product Purpose

Ghost Mobile is the daily driver companion for a personal AI that lives on hardware the owner controls. It pairs over a secure QR flow, chats with streaming responses, manages approvals, goals, and automations, and stays honest offline. Success is trust: every answer says where it ran, every action shows runtime evidence, nothing pretends.

## Brand Personality

Warm, quiet, premium, human. Calm companion, never a terminal, never a SaaS dashboard. Light first, midnight only where conversation needs warmth.

## Anti-references

Terminal green on black hacker consoles. Neon on black crypto voice apps. SaaS hero metric dashboards with gradient numbers. Identical icon card grids. Glassmorphic floating cards. AI voice apps that hide where compute runs or fake offline ability.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence over claims: show where an answer ran and what actually happened.
2. Calm carries the task: one primary action per screen, quiet hierarchy, no decoration that competes with content.
3. Honest about limits: offline, unconfigured, and busy states say so plainly with a path forward.
4. Consistency is trust: the same button, bubble, and status vocabulary on every screen.
5. Private by construction: credentials stay in secure storage, the UI never leaks secrets or message content into notifications.
6. One mental model per job: the owner expresses intent in conversation; Ghost files it. Internal nouns (routine, automation, scheduled item) may appear as quiet badges, never as a filing decision the owner must make.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA for text contrast. Full keyboard and screen reader labels on all controls. Reduced motion respected everywhere. Touch targets 44px minimum.
